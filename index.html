Research

Domain generalization for generalized category discovery in remote sensing

Fall 2026 – present
Graduation project, Sabancı University · Prof. Erchan Aptoula

Generalized category discovery when test images come from domains not seen during training. I'm reproducing recent GCD and DG-GCD methods and comparing them under the same datasets and evaluation settings, before moving to a new method in the second half of the project.

  • Built a GCD pipeline with DINO features, k-means and semi-supervised k-means, contrastive training, Hungarian matching, and Old/New/All accuracy.
  • Currently studying DG2CD-Net, HiDISC, and related work.

Sparse matrix–vector multiplication

Summer 2026 – present
Research project, Sabancı University · with guidance from Prof. Kamer Kaya

Implementations and optimizations of SpMV across different hardware and execution settings, with recent work focused on comparing newer SpMV methods in a common benchmarking environment.

  • Sequential CSR, OpenMP, CUDA, MPI, and hybrid CPU–GPU versions of SpMV.
  • Compared matrix reorderings (RCM, AMD, GPS, and graph-based methods) and their effect on performance.
  • Benchmarking code across sparse matrices, thread counts, and execution settings.

Fully homomorphic encryption for matrix multiplication

Jul 2025 – Feb 2026
PURE research intern, later research assistant, Sabancı University · Prof. Erkay Savaş

Matrix multiplication under the CKKS scheme using Microsoft SEAL. Presented at the Sabancı University PURE research symposium.

  • Implemented and compared Halevi–Shoup and bicyclic encoding in C++.
  • Benchmarked runtime, ciphertext rotations, and multiplicative depth across matrix sizes.
  • Tested batching and chunking for encrypted matrix–vector multiplication.
